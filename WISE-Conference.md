# WISE Conference: International Conference on Web Information Systems Engineering

## Overview

**WISE** (Web Information Systems Engineering) is a premier international conference series that has established itself as a leading forum for researchers, professionals, and industrial practitioners to share cutting-edge knowledge and innovations in web technologies, methodologies, and applications. The conference celebrates **25+ years of excellence** and continues to advance the field of web information systems at a global scale.

### Conference Details

- **Full Name**: International Conference on Web Information Systems Engineering
- **Editions**: 26th edition in 2025 (WISE 2025)
- **2025 Location**: Marrakech, Morocco
- **2025 Dates**: December 15-18, 2025
- **Previous Notable Edition**: WISE 2024 (25th edition) held in Doha, Qatar
- **Series Start**: 2000 (Hong Kong)
- **Publication Venue**: Springer Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS)

## Mission & Scope

WISE provides an **international forum for researchers, professionals, and industrial practitioners** to:

1. **Share knowledge and insights** in the rapidly growing area of Web technologies
2. **Advance research** in web information systems and their applications
3. **Foster collaboration** between academia and industry
4. **Address emerging challenges** in web-scale computing and data management

The conference is particularly committed to **maximizing the positive impact of the Web on society** through research that touches the lives of people in meaningful ways.

## Core Research Areas

WISE encompasses a broad spectrum of research topics organized into several key categories:

### Web Information Systems & Architecture
- Web Information Systems design and engineering
- Web-scale Distributed and Cloud Computing
- Web infrastructures and architectures
- Content Delivery Networks (CDNs)
- Web tools and visualization

### Data & Knowledge Management
- **Web Data Models**: Evaluation, metrics, and performance standards
- **Web Information Retrieval**: Search engines, text analytics, and ranking algorithms
- **Linked Open Data**: Semantic web technologies and ontologies
- **Web Mining**: Clickstream analytics, sequential/session-based analysis
- **Web Warehousing**: Data integration and aggregation

### Machine Learning & Intelligence
- **Machine Learning for the Web**: Deep learning applications, neural networks
- **Web Agents and Web Intelligence**: Autonomous systems and decision-making
- **Recommender Systems**: Personalization and preference learning
- **Graph Learning**: Knowledge graphs and network analysis
- **Data Science and AI Technology**: Advanced analytics and artificial intelligence

### Social & Human Factors
- **Social Web Models and Analysis**: Social network analysis, community detection
- **Human Factors and Social Issues**: Digital inclusion, accessibility
- **Crowdsourcing Applications**: Human computation and collective intelligence
- **Rich Web UI and HCI**: User interface design, interaction patterns

### Security, Privacy & Trust
- **Web Security and Trust Management**: Authentication, encryption, threat detection
- **Privacy-Preserving Systems**: Federated learning, differential privacy
- **Web Privacy Management**: GDPR compliance, data protection
- **Identity Management**: Access control and authentication

### Advanced Topics
- **Semantic Web**: RDF, OWL, and semantic technologies
- **Deep/Hidden Web**: Accessing non-indexed web content
- **Blockchain Technologies**: Decentralized systems and smart contracts
- **IoT and Web of Things**: Integration of internet-connected devices
- **Edge Computing**: Distributed computing at network edge
- **Trustworthy ML**: Fairness, transparency, and responsible AI

### Web Applications
- **E-Commerce**: Online marketplaces, auctions, and negotiation systems
- **E-Government**: Digital public services
- **E-Learning**: Online education platforms
- **Web-based Business Processes**: Workflow management and services
- **Web Services**: API design, service composition, microservices

## Research Track Details

### Submission Guidelines

**WISE 2025 Research Track** solicits **high-quality, original papers** focusing on:
- Important research problems and challenges
- Novel solutions with significant impact
- Both theoretical and empirical contributions
- Reproducible results with publicly available code (preferred)

### Paper Requirements

- Papers must present **significant novel results**
- Double-blind peer review process
- Accepted papers published in **Springer LNCS** proceedings
- Best papers invited for journal publication in:
  - **World Wide Web Journal** (Springer)
  - **Data Science and Engineering** (Springer)

### Review Criteria

Papers are evaluated on:
1. **Novelty**: Original contributions to the field
2. **Technical Quality**: Sound methodology and rigorous evaluation
3. **Clarity**: Clear presentation and well-written
4. **Significance**: Potential impact on research and practice
5. **Reproducibility**: Availability of code and datasets
